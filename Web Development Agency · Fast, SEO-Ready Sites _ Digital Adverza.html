~/faq

Before you ask.

The questions every website client asks us first.

01How long does it take to build a website?

A standard business website takes 7–14 days. E-commerce stores or custom web apps may take 3–6 weeks depending on complexity. We always agree on a timeline upfront.

02Will my website work on mobile?

Absolutely. Every website we build is mobile-first and fully responsive, tested on all screen sizes and browsers before delivery.

03Do you provide hosting and domain?

Yes, we can assist with domain registration and hosting setup. We recommend reliable hosting providers and can manage the entire setup on your behalf.

04Can I update the website myself after launch?

Yes. We build with easy-to-use CMS (WordPress or custom admin panels) so you can update content, add products, and manage pages without any coding knowledge.
